# HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights

## Overview
This end-to-end HR Analytics project transforms raw employee data into actionable business insights using **Excel, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI**. The project focuses on analyzing workforce trends, employee attrition, salary patterns, promotion history, and work-life balance to support data-driven HR decision-making.

## Objective
To help organizations improve employee retention, optimize workforce planning, and enhance organizational performance through advanced analytics and interactive dashboarding.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Excel** – Data cleaning, preprocessing, and dashboard creation  
- **SQL (MySQL)** – Data extraction, transformation, aggregations, and KPI analysis  
- **Tableau** – Interactive visualizations and workforce trend analysis  
- **Power BI** – Advanced dashboard development, KPI tracking, filters, and business intelligence  

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Employee Count:** 50,000  
- **Attrition Rate:** 50.21%  
- **Average Salary:** ₹26.02K  
- **Average Age:** 38.97 Years  
- **Average Hourly Rate:** 115.43  
- **Work-Life Balance Score:** 2.50  

## Key Insights
- Highest employee attrition observed in the **50–60 age group**
- **Research & Development** and **Sales** departments experience the highest turnover
- Workforce distribution is nearly balanced across genders
- Salary levels and promotion history significantly impact retention
- Work-life balance varies across different job roles and departments

## Dashboard Features
- Department-wise Attrition Analysis  
- Gender-based Workforce Distribution  
- Monthly Income vs Attrition Trends  
- Work-Life Balance by Job Role  
- Promotion vs Retention Analysis  
- Interactive Filters and Slicers for dynamic workforce segmentation  

## Project Workflow
**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → SQL Analysis → Dashboard Creation → Business Insights**

## Business Impact
This project enables organizations to:
- Improve employee retention strategies  
- Identify high-risk attrition segments  
- Optimize workforce planning  
- Enhance strategic HR decision-making  
- Support long-term business growth

The HR Analytics Dashboard demonstrates the power of data analytics in transforming HR operations by uncovering critical workforce trends and delivering actionable insights.
